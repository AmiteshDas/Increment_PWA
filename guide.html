The idea

Every session becomes a point

Run, read, write, whatever you're building. Log it as an increment and rate two things: how hard it felt, and whether you'd want to do it again. Multiply those together and you get an Effort Score. Try it:

How the graph plots

Ten sessions, one shape

Each increment adds a point. String enough points together and a curve appears on its own. Drag the slider through a sample arc and watch it happen.

Reading your arc

Four states, no judgment

BuildingJust getting going. Not enough sessions yet to see a shape.
RisingRecent sessions are working harder than the ones before them.
SteadyYou found your level and you're holding it.
DecliningEasing off. Usually just means you got good at this.
In practice

It sneaks into how you talk

"I'm 12 increments into my running arc, it's plateauing."

Translation: steady, consistent, no longer a struggle.
